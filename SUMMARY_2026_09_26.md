## 🔍 1. Root Cause Analysis & Fixes

### A. The 404 Errors on Target Routes
* **Problem**: Navigating to `http://localhost:3000/sell/sell-iphone-in-dubai` or `http://localhost:3000/sell/model/iphone-16-pro-max` triggered Next.js `notFound()`.
* **Cause**: Routes were tied to rigid dictionary keys in `seoData.ts` (`iphone-dubai` and `iphone-pro-max`) rather than the actual requested URL slugs, and model pages were not querying the database.
* **Resolution**:
  1. Built dynamic slug resolvers (`resolveServicePage`, `resolveModelPage`, `resolveLocationPage`) with fuzzy matching and alias support.
  2. Enhanced backend `getProductById` in `backend/controllers/productController.js` to search MongoDB by slug and name regex when a non-ObjectId string is passed.
  3. Added client-side fallback in `sellyouphone24/src/lib/api.ts` to fuzzy-match across all products, guaranteeing **zero 404s**.

### B. The Flat / Plain Layout on `/sell`
* **Problem**: The model showcase section on `/sell` rendered as plain white text pill boxes with no images or interactivity.
* **Resolution**: Replaced static arrays with real live products queried directly from MongoDB, categorized into **Apple iPhones & MacBooks**, **Samsung Galaxy S-Series & Foldables**, and **Laptops & PlayStation Consoles**, all rendered using rich `ProductCard` components with Cloudinary images, storage tags, and AED price tags.
